Common questions

Straight answers to the questions customers ask.

What does Shore GTM do?

We help leadership teams find what's holding growth back, decide what needs to change, and put the right solution into motion—using AI where it genuinely improves results.

Do we have to start with the GTM Diagnostic?

No. If the problem is already clear, we can start with strategy, prioritization, advisory, or build. The Diagnostic is for when growth is off plan and the reason is not obvious.

Do you implement AI tools, or just provide strategy?

Both. Strategy through execution. When the solution needs to be built—workflows, CRM, scoring, reporting, automation—we can stay involved and deliver it.

How do we know if we need your help?

If revenue is underperforming, teams disagree on the cause, AI activity isn't producing measurable outcomes, or you know what needs to change but lack the team to build it—that's a fit conversation worth having.

How do you measure success and ROI?

We establish a starting point before work begins and measure whether the business improved against defined outcomes—not activity metrics.

We've tried AI tools before and didn't see results. Why would this be different?

Most AI initiatives stall from sequencing, not technology. We start with the business constraint, then apply AI only where it improves that specific outcome.

Do you replace our existing GTM leadership?

No. We advise and augment. Shore works alongside your leadership team to sharpen decisions and accelerate execution.

Can you work with our existing technology and partners?

Yes. We build around the tools you already use and connect them more effectively rather than forcing a rip-and-replace.

What does AI Advisory include?

Ongoing senior guidance on GTM AI priorities, scale/pause/stop decisions, and review of results across sales, marketing, CS, and RevOps—without hiring a full-time AI executive.
