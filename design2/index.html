The problem

Slow revenue growth is a headache. And everyone has a different opinion about the cause.

A missed number is easy to see but the reason behind it usually isn't. Revenue is produced by an interconnected system—and fixing the most visible problem often does not fix the business.

01

Revenue is not growing fast enough

02

Teams see different problems

03

AI without diagnosis

Employees use AI daily while leadership is asked what it's producing. More activity doesn't clarify the underlying constraint.

The Shore GTM approach

Our job isn't to find somewhere to use AI. It's to find what's holding performance back—and whether AI can help produce a better result.
