About Shore GTM

Operator-led AI executive advisory for measurable business performance.

Shore GTM was founded by Patrick Rooney, a 3× founder with two exits, and former CEO, CMO, GM, and GTM executive.

For more than 25 years, Patrick has built companies, led commercial teams, made investment decisions, missed numbers, changed plans, launched products, managed growth, and been accountable for the result.

Shore GTM brings that operator perspective to companies trying to improve GTM performance and decide where AI can genuinely make the business better.

Our point of view

Business judgment first. Technology where it helps.

Operator-led

The work is led by someone who has been responsible for revenue, people, investment, customers, and company performance—not only the recommendation.

Evidence-first

Shore uses the company's own data to test assumptions and identify where performance is actually breaking.

GTM-wide

We look across the full commercial system rather than assume the problem belongs to sales, marketing, or customer success.

Strategy through execution

When the solution needs to be built, Shore can stay involved and turn the strategy into something the team can actually use.

The gap we address

AI is changing the work. It doesn't change the fundamentals.

Employees are already figuring out how to use AI—writing, researching, analyzing, preparing, automating. That is a good thing.

But companies do not win because employees produce more content, summarize meetings faster, or use more AI tools. They win because they acquire and retain the right customers, make good decisions, execute consistently, improve margins, and create value customers are willing to pay for.

That is the gap Shore GTM was built to address.

A note of honesty

Shore GTM is not for everyone.

Fit matters as much to us as it does to you. We are probably not the right partner if any of the following describe what you are looking for.

You want AI activity without accountability to measurable business outcomes.

There is no executive sponsor willing to support decisions, adoption, and change.

You are unwilling to define measurable business outcomes before the work begins.

You need activity and optics—not operational change.

You need a vendor to simply “add AI” to existing work, rather than a partner to help define what should change.
